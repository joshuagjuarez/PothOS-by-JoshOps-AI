documentation. It does not contain the standalone PothOS runtime or grant a
license to private code, private ArcheAgents, memories, voices or credentials.

related paid services and use in a person's own income-producing work remains

scope is unresolved. Engineering, privacy review and release preparation continue.

Copyright in new material is retained pending its explicit licensing terms.

as source-available rather than as conventional open source. The
[Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd) permits commercial use and
redistribution.

## Enforcement boundaries

A project can govern its own participation and the services it actually operates.
License termination depends on valid applicable terms and the facts of a breach.
No ability to remotely disable every independently operated local copy is
promised. No private-data collection or mandatory online control mechanism is
introduced by this policy.

Third-party code, model weights and assets retain their own terms. They must be
reviewed before any bundle is distributed. Project names and marks must not be
used to imply an endorsement that does not exist.
