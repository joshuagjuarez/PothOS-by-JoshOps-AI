# Candidate licensing structure — not an applied grant

Recommended candidate: use the unmodified **Apache License 2.0** for the exact,
reviewed list of reusable L4 source files in the eventual public release. Ship
the official license text and required notices with source and binaries. Keep
third-party licenses and notices. Record the actual rights holder; do not invent
an LLC or assign another contributor's copyright.

This candidate keeps a familiar license for developers, permits commercial and
noncommercial reuse, and carries the license's patent terms. Its trademark
provision does not give a general right to present a fork as an official JoshOps
AI release. See the [governing text](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
Do not alter that text by appending a prohibition and still label it Apache-2.0.

## Rights boundary

The license would apply only after the rights holder accepts the exact scope and
it is published with the grant. A private staging document is not that act.
The already published 14 documentation/template paths retain their existing
Apache-2.0 grant under the
[earlier scope](https://github.com/joshuagjuarez/joshuagjuarez/blob/a58602bff3cf5243faf0cdf70a857904bf3e10e0/docs/pothos/LICENSE_SCOPE.md).
New runtime rights remain unresolved under the current public
[license status](https://github.com/joshuagjuarez/PothOS-by-JoshOps-AI/blob/main/LICENSE_STATUS.md).

Reusable memory, identity, recovery and agent mechanisms belong in the product
scope where rights permit. Joshua's corpus, named agents' populated profiles,
private venture records, OAuth sessions, credentials, source receipts containing
personal data, device bindings and voices do not become public because their
supporting mechanisms do. Model weights and media require separate applicable
rights; do not assume a code license covers them.

## Protection and the unresolved commercial restriction

Joshua's purpose is to protect people from exploitation. The project can require
privacy-preserving contributions, moderate its own spaces, and set terms for
services it operates. Those controls are drafted separately in this packet.
They must not be presented as a universal ability to revoke all offline copies.

Conventional open source permits commercial use and cannot exclude fields of
endeavor. A binding ban on resale, profit, or specified uses would require a
different, accurately described source-available licensing strategy. It cannot
be silently added to this open-source candidate. This is the precise remaining
rights choice preserved for Arthur and Joshua; engineering and candidate
preparation continue without pretending it has already been settled.
Source: [Open Source Definition, sections 1, 5 and 6](https://opensource.org/osd).

## Contributor terms proposed for the release

Contributors retain ownership of their contributions and submit covered changes
under the same applicable license as the files changed. They must have the
right to contribute, disclose third-party material and preserve its notices.
AI-assisted changes need the same review, provenance and tests as other changes.
No assignment of all future ideas, private chats or personal cognitive records
is required. A separate optional commercial engagement must identify its work,
rights and payment terms rather than being inferred from a GitHub contribution.

Arthur's review should bind the exact source inventory, rights evidence,
dependency/model/asset notices, platform terms and current licensing decision
to the existing release. This packet is a concrete draft for that review; it
does not claim a legal opinion or guarantee enforcement.
