# Proposed official community and service commitments

PothOS exists to help people pursue their own purposes. A person's memory,
worldview, relationships and language are not an advertising inventory or a
resource that community contributors acquire by maintaining the software.

For JoshOps AI-operated services and project spaces, proposed rules prohibit:

- Taking or exposing another person's private corpus, credentials, voice or
  identity without authorization.
- Covert profiling, targeted manipulation using private cognitive records,
  impersonation, credential theft, harassment or unauthorized surveillance.
- Hiding data transmission, making cloud processing mandatory without saying so,
  or representing a paid/provider-connected route as wholly local.
- Publishing private helpdesk payloads, logs or screenshots as public examples.
- Claiming Joshua or JoshOps AI endorses an independent fork without permission.

Participation and managed-service access can be reviewed or suspended for
violations under the applicable terms. Keep reasons, evidence and a way to
request review; protect complainants' private information. Do not silently
delete a person's local memory or introduce a remote disable switch into their
independent installation as a community-enforcement mechanism.

These rules govern the services and spaces to which they apply. They do not
change the rights of independently licensed source code or guarantee prevention
of every misuse. Existing legal rights remain separate.

## Product acceptance obligations

Verify before claiming each property is implemented:

1. The operator can inspect, export, correct and delete their own retained data
   under the application's actual storage/retention contract.
2. Connector access is explicit, scoped and revocable. Credentials stay in
   protected custody; model prompts and public logs receive references, not keys.
3. Default diagnostic submissions contain versions, operation/result identifiers,
   error codes and reproduction steps. Private text or recordings require a
   deliberate scoped attachment. Beta participation is not training consent.
4. Offline-supported workflows remain usable without a flagship subscription.
   Provider-dependent workflows clearly show that dependency and any cost.
5. Backup/restore is tested without relying on one irreplaceable machine or a
   proprietary chat history. Restore does not bypass a provider's authentication.
6. Account switching, revocation and restarts preserve the correct principal and
   refuse another account's corpus, fleet and receipts.

"Powered by PothOS" may describe a factual use of the software under a published
brand policy. It must not imply official certification or endorsement. This
draft grants no additional trademark or certification rights.
