# PothOS: free local AI infrastructure for people everywhere

**Community announcement | November 7, 2026**

JoshOps AI is opening PothOS to community development with a clear purpose:
make infrastructure for local AI freely available so people everywhere can
build their own ArcheAgents, preserve their knowledge and work, and retain
control over their digital lives.

The ambition is human representation. People should be able to shape a
continuing digital agent around their own intentions, chosen responsibilities
and permitted information. They should be able to understand its actions,
correct its assumptions, choose its tools and stop its work. Their history
should remain useful when a conversation, model, device or provider changes.

## A shared foundation people can build on

Founder Joshua Gustafson-Juarez will continue developing his own ArcheAgents
while inviting others to help develop the shared infrastructure for theirs.
The community effort is intended to carry the project further than one person
can take it alone.

PothOS brings together a practical set of goals: local AI, recoverable memory,
work that can continue across sessions, understandable permissions and tools
that remain accountable to the person using them. An ArcheAgent's identity and
history should belong to its continuing relationship with that person, while
the models and execution tools supporting it can change.

Local AI sovereignty means meaningful control over where information is kept,
which resources an agent may use, what leaves a person's environment and how
work can be recovered. Supported local configurations must make any external
service dependencies clear. People should be able to choose connections and
collaboration without giving up control of their private information.

## Free of charge, a worldwide purpose

**Official PothOS software will be free of charge. Always.**

The purpose reaches beyond any one company, country or technical community.
PothOS is an invitation to build infrastructure that helps people understand
themselves, pursue their work, support one another and collaborate across
communities and countries. The aim is to bring people together while preserving
each person's independence and representation.

The commitment to free official software applies to PothOS. Hardware,
optional third-party services and model licenses have their own requirements
and costs. The project's prospective runtime terms are documented in
[license status](../LICENSE_STATUS.md).

## What is available today

The public GitHub project currently provides the mission, design requirements,
roadmap, accessibility and privacy requirements, and a contribution process.
Standalone runtime source and an independently installable local distribution
remain in preparation. The current release status is maintained
[here](RELEASE_STATUS.md).

The next shared milestone is a supported local installation that a new person
can use to complete a useful task, preserve and correct a synthetic memory,
restart, and recover the same state with understandable permission and network
behavior.

## Help build it

Local AI developers, accessibility contributors, privacy reviewers, technical
writers and people testing everyday workflows are invited to contribute.
Useful work includes reliable installation and recovery, voice and keyboard
access, clear permission boundaries, and continuity that can be demonstrated.

Start with the public documentation and propose one concrete improvement with
a way to verify that it helps. Use synthetic examples and respect each
participant's private information.
