# Support JoshOps AI

Help build local AI computing that people can use on their own devices, with their own accounts, memory and tools.

JoshOps AI is founded and developed by Joshua David Gustafson-Juarez in Houston, Texas. The application contains each participant's PothOS and ArcheAgent. Official software is free for personal, nonprofit and ordinary business use. Sponsorship supports development; it does not buy access to someone's private memory or unlock a paid software tier.

## Contribute your skills

[Join the development effort](https://github.com/joshuagjuarez/PothOS-by-JoshOps-AI/issues/new?template=developer-participation.yml). Tell us your platform, area of interest and whether you represent yourself or a team. Start with one reproducible improvement: installation, accessibility, UI, local-model adapters, workflow cards, documentation, account isolation or recovery. Maintainers review official updates before release.

[Discuss ideas and find collaborators](https://github.com/joshuagjuarez/PothOS-by-JoshOps-AI/discussions). Report reproducible faults through [issue forms](https://github.com/joshuagjuarez/PothOS-by-JoshOps-AI/issues/new/choose). Submit vulnerabilities through [private security reporting](https://github.com/joshuagjuarez/PothOS-by-JoshOps-AI/security/advisories/new).

## Sponsor development

We welcome individual and organizational support, donated test hardware, compute credits, documentation help and infrastructure partnerships. Support goes toward engineering time, cross-platform testing, accessible interfaces, packaging and reliable local operation.

[Start a sponsorship conversation](https://github.com/joshuagjuarez/PothOS-by-JoshOps-AI/discussions/new?category=general). State the kind of support you can offer and the work you want to help sustain. Keep private financial and identity information out of public discussions. Private partnership and investment inquiries can use the [company contact page](https://joshops.ai/support#contact).

GitHub Sponsors payment enrollment is being completed. This page currently accepts proposals, not payments. The repository Sponsor button opens this page; it must not be mistaken for an active checkout. Once GitHub verifies the account and payout setup, the official payment destination will be linked here. Sponsorship does not promise equity, financial returns, preferential access to private data or guaranteed delivery dates.

## Custom work and research partnerships

We welcome proposals for scoped deployment and integration work, custom workflows and adapters, model evaluation or fine-tuning, training and workshops, and collaborations with research or education labs. These are conversations about separately agreed services and deliverables; they do not introduce a software license fee. Feasibility, rights to data/models, costs, scope and acceptance are agreed for each engagement.

Use the [company contact page](https://joshops.ai/support#contact) for private commercial, strategic partnership or investment discussions. No equity, price, financial return or acquisition terms are offered by this page. Public sponsorship requests can remain in Discussions.

## Build together

Personal, Entrepreneur, Enterprise and Developer are ways to use one environment. Contributors can improve the adapters and workflows they use without receiving the Founder's accounts, private corpus or device credentials. Google Workspace integration uses each participant's authorized accounts. Further provider adapters are developed and verified individually.
