# Mission: carry meaning, reduce noise

PothOS is intended to provide continuity that preserves source, meaning,
corrections and human authority.

The practical problem is familiar. A person develops ideas and obligations
across conversations, applications, machines and years. A new session can lose
the context, repeat a corrected assumption or make the person explain everything
again. More generated text can add to the noise.

PothOS is intended to help that person recover useful signal, retain the history
behind a decision and continue the next appropriate work. Its humanitarian
purpose is to increase people's ability to understand themselves, develop their
own work and help other people.

## Cognitive sovereignty

People should retain control over their own cognitive information: memories,
intentions, corrections, relationships and unfinished work. Vendor access to
that private signal must not become the price of participation.

Local sovereignty includes carrying permitted information across one's own
devices and infrastructure. Portability, recovery and human control belong
together. A local installation must disclose any external service it uses and
must not silently replace a local selection with a cloud call.

## Continuity that can be checked

The framework's release evidence should demonstrate that a new session or
device can recover the same admitted record, its corrections and its current
state. Familiar language alone cannot demonstrate that result.

Sources, interpretations, proposed plans and observed outcomes need separate
standing. A later correction should remain connected to what it corrects.
Permission to read one resource should not become access to every resource.

An ArcheAgent can carry a user-chosen identity and responsibilities across
supported sessions. Its name, role, current model and actual permissions are
separate concerns. The person remains able to inspect, correct and stop work.

That continuity includes the ArcheAgent's source-backed profile and history,
its permitted corpus, and unfinished obligations from earlier days. Daily review
must recover existing work and its actual state before proposing more. Observable
metacognitive checks should revisit assumptions, evidence and outcomes through
the existing runtime owners. The
[continuing ArcheAgent requirements](CONTINUING_ARCHEAGENTS.md) describe these
mechanisms and the evidence needed for a recurring service claim.

These are product requirements, not a claim that every configuration is
implemented or qualified today. The [release status](RELEASE_STATUS.md) and
[roadmap](ROADMAP.md) identify the remaining work.

## Community purpose

PothOS is a free community project. Participation should improve usefulness,
accessibility and accountability. The project opposes deception, coercion,
unauthorized surveillance and exploitation of people's private signal.

The founder's no-charging direction and its prospective licensing treatment are
recorded in [license status](../LICENSE_STATUS.md). Project ethics do not
retroactively rewrite already-granted licenses.

The public work supports others creating their own environments. It does not
distribute the founder's private identities, memories, voice assets or estate.
