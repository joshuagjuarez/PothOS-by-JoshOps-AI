# Community principles

PothOS exists to support cognitive sovereignty, shared learning, accessibility
and useful human development. Participation should help people recover meaning
and carry their own work.

## Conduct and contribution boundaries

- Do not harvest, sell, profile or expose people's private cognitive information.
- Do not contribute deception, coercion, unauthorized surveillance, impersonation
  or mechanisms for exploiting people.
- Respect explicit permission, source custody and personal/tenant boundaries.
- Do not harass contributors or publish someone else's private records.
- State limitations, corrections and evidence honestly.
- Preserve the project's names and do not imply endorsements or compatibility
  that have not been established.

Maintainers may decline contributions and moderate participation in the project
spaces they control. That moderation is separate from the rights granted by a
software license and from control of another person's local installation.

The founder's direction against charging for PothOS is recorded in
[license status](LICENSE_STATUS.md). These principles do not secretly add
restrictions to earlier Apache-2.0 documentation or act as a runtime license.
