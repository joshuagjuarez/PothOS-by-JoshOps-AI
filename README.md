# PothOS

**Your own ArcheAgents. Your devices. Your continuity.**

PothOS by JoshOps AI is a community-led project for local AI sovereignty:
helping people keep their knowledge, tools and ongoing work under their own
control across devices and models.

Founded and designed by **Joshua David Gustafson-Juarez**. Contributors retain
credit for their own work.

## What we are building

- **Continuity:** recover original sources, corrections and unfinished work when
  a conversation, model or device changes.
- **Personal agency:** create your own ArcheAgents, profiles, roles and working
  environment.
- **Connected tools:** use supported native, CLI, API and MCP adapters through
  your own accounts and permissions.
- **Local control:** inspect, correct, export and recover your information, with
  external processing made explicit.
- **Accessibility:** develop useful voice, keyboard and screen-reader workflows.

The shared product provides reusable mechanisms. Every person supplies their own
identity, data, devices and permitted connections. Maintainers' personal agents,
populated profiles, private ventures, credentials and infrastructure are not
product defaults or public release content.

See the [product contract](docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md) and
[continuity requirements](docs/CONTINUING_ARCHEAGENTS.md).

## Current release

The first native beta is focused on **sign-in, assistant connections, private
continuity and shared collaboration**. Interactive conversation and additional
device integrations retain their own development and acceptance work.

This repository currently contains project and contributor documentation.
The standalone runtime source and signed native customer installer are not
released here. The existing hosted entry, connection kit and future native
packages are separate deliverables; [release status](docs/RELEASE_STATUS.md)
records their actual availability.
