# PothOS by JoshOps AI

## Free local AI infrastructure. Your own ArcheAgents. Human control.

PothOS exists to make infrastructure for local AI freely available to people
around the world, so they can build their own **ArcheAgents** and retain control
over their knowledge, tools and digital lives. The purpose is human
representation and cooperation across communities and countries.

[Read the November 7 community announcement](docs/PRESS_RELEASE.md).

PothOS is being developed so a human and their digital intelligence can carry
meaningful work across infrastructure, platforms, software, devices and models
without repeatedly losing context or surrendering control of personal information.

The purpose is to reduce noise, recover useful signal, preserve what a person
actually meant and help them develop themselves and help others.
JoshOps AI is the project identity; PothOS is the product.

**Current contents: mission, design and contributor documentation.**
The independently installable runtime source has not been released. This repository
does not contain a model installer or a working standalone PothOS distribution.

## The commitment

Official PothOS software is to remain free of charge. The founder's direction
opposes charging people for PothOS and exploiting their private cognitive
information. People should control their memory, tools, permissions and the
movement of their information across their own environment.

Prospective runtime terms are being defined to reflect that direction. This is
a planned community source release, with restrictions still under review.
Read [license status](LICENSE_STATUS.md) before relying on a reuse permission.
Earlier Apache-2.0 documentation grants remain effective for their covered
material; they do not license the private runtime.

## What continuity means here

- Recover original sources, corrections and unfinished obligations after a session,
  model or device changes.
- Preserve the distinction between a person's words, an interpretation and a result.
- Keep personal, family, organizational and community information within its
  appropriate permissions.
- Make memory inspection, correction, portability and recovery understandable.
- Let people work with their own **ArcheAgents**, with identities and responsibilities
  they choose, while preserving human authority.

An ArcheAgent is part of that continuity framework. No personal ArcheAgent,
private voice, memory or founder environment is included here.

Read [mission and continuity](docs/MISSION.md) and
[cognitive privacy](docs/PRIVACY.md).

Continuity also requires an attributable past and a growing permitted corpus,
not only the result of a single task. The
[continuing ArcheAgent requirements](docs/CONTINUING_ARCHEAGENTS.md) connect the
profile, source-backed history, daily recovery of earlier work and observable
metacognitive review. They include qualification criteria for a recurring runtime;
this documentation does not establish an always-on public service.

## Build with the community

Local AI developers, accessibility contributors, technical writers and people
testing real workflows are welcome. The immediate work is:

1. Review continuity and privacy requirements using synthetic examples.
2. Prepare reusable code with clear ownership, dependencies and release terms.
3. Qualify an independent local installation and memory recovery.
4. Improve voice and accessibility: keyboard access, screen-reader support,
   speech input/output, recording controls and device behavior.
5. Document a small supported configuration before making wider compatibility claims.

Native voice work exists in the underlying project. Its public packaging and
device qualification remain work to complete. See [accessibility](docs/ACCESSIBILITY.md),
[the roadmap](docs/ROADMAP.md) and [release status](docs/RELEASE_STATUS.md).

Developers using Ollama, llama.cpp, LM Studio, LocalAI, OpenAI, Claude, Grok and
other ecosystems are welcome to contribute relevant expertise. This invitation
does not claim integration, endorsement or support from those projects.
