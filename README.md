# JoshOps AI

**Your PothOS. Your ArcheAgent. Your devices and ongoing work.**

JoshOps AI is one application and operating stack across its supported desktop, touch, web, CLI, TUI and MCP surfaces. PothOS provides each person's continuity and workspace inside that application; their ArcheAgent carries their own identity, responsibilities and permitted work.

Founded and designed by **Joshua David Gustafson-Juarez**. The project welcomes community development for local AI sovereignty, accessibility and useful work in personal, nonprofit, business and developer environments.

## Community release

This repository and its release downloads are now public. The application people download is **JoshOps AI**; PothOS and their ArcheAgent live inside it. Founder accounts, private memory and credentials remain private.

Mac with Apple silicon now has a signed, Apple-notarized **1.0.10 connected preview**. Android APK/AAB 1.0.8 are signed and verified. The Windows 1.0.8 installer now has a verified publisher signature; Windows Authenticode verification remains pending. [Download the Mac preview](https://github.com/joshuagjuarez/PothOS-by-JoshOps-AI/releases/download/v1.0.10-beta.1/JoshOps-AI-1.0.10-macos-arm64-notarized.dmg) or [choose your platform](docs/DOWNLOADS.md). These previews connect to the existing account service; complete local CLI/model packages and store approvals have their own release status in the [artifact manifest](docs/release-artifacts.json).

The website is the doorway to the existing master application. Platform builds reuse the common application and preserve device-specific capabilities. A connected recipient package does not contain the founder's personal corpus, credentials or fleet bindings.

A supported phone is also a local computing node. Its native Foundation adapter and local workspace are distinct from the fleet connection. See [Phone nodes and local engines](docs/PHONE_NODES.md) for current support, measured status and adapter contribution boundaries.

## One environment for your work

- **Personal:** free continuity for notes, files, ideas and projects.
- **Enterprise:** the $20/month household membership model for participation in JoshOps AI operations, organizational support and scoped implementation work. Its intended ownership, profit-sharing and property-participation structure remains under formalization; enrollment awaits the actual terms.
- **Developer:** free participation in the published GitHub materials and development program, under their applicable licenses and account permissions.

These are three views of the same application. Shared HelpDesk and Huddle stay available across them, and Halo is an optional quiet view. Available services follow the user's connected accounts, enrolled devices and permissions. [Connection and HTTPS setup](docs/CONNECTIONS.md) explains hosted assistants and local CLI clients.

## Build with us

Use the [developer participation form](https://github.com/joshuagjuarez/PothOS-by-JoshOps-AI/issues/new?template=developer-participation.yml) to identify your platform, contribution area and whether you represent yourself or a team. An issue records a request; the maintainer assigns approved work and access separately.

Contribute UI improvements, reusable workflows, adapters, documentation, accessibility checks and reproducible fixes through issues and pull requests. Official updates are reviewed and verified before distribution. Each participant keeps their own accounts, private data and permissions.

For project support, sponsorship proposals, hardware or development collaboration, use [Discussions](https://github.com/joshuagjuarez/PothOS-by-JoshOps-AI/discussions). Bugs and testing requests go through the shared issue forms. [Support the project](docs/SUPPORT-THE-PROJECT.md) through engineering, testing, hardware, compute credits or sponsorship proposals. GitHub payment enrollment is being prepared; sponsorship never unlocks a paid software tier.

[Verify publisher signatures](docs/PUBLISHER_SIGNATURES.md) for the current downloads. The existing release key verifies the exact files and rejects changed bytes; platform trust and store approval retain their own status.
